Frequently Asked Questions

What is Devil May Cry 5? +

Devil May Cry 5 is an action game developed by Capcom. Players fight demons using stylish combat, weapons and special abilities.

Who are the main characters? +

The main playable characters are Dante, Nero and V. Vergil is also an important character in the story.

When was DMC5 released? +

Devil May Cry 5 was released on March 8, 2019.
